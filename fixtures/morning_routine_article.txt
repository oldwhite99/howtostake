Every morning begins with my alarm. I turn it off and get out of bed. The room is quiet. First, I open the curtains and stretch my arms. Then it's time to make my bed.

In the bathroom, I grab my towel, brush my teeth, and wash my face. After that, I apply moisturizer, put on my clothes, and tie my shoelaces.

Breakfast is simple. I pour some coffee and toast some bread. A banana is on the table, ready to eat. I slice it and spread butter on my toast.

After breakfast, it's time to pack my bag. My phone is on the table, so I check it. Next, I fill my water bottle and zip my jacket. With everything ready, I lock the door and take the stairs.

Outside, the walk to the bus stop is short. I wait for the bus. When it arrives, I tap my card and find a seat. The city is busy, so I look out the window. Then I plug in my earphones and listen to music.

At my stop, I step off the bus. The day is just beginning!
